The story behind MR Techworks

How I got started

Hi, my name is Matthew Roupp. I am a Computer Science student from SUNY Empire State University, I am a father, and someone who genuinely enjoys helping people work through problems. MR Techworks is where I bring those strengths together into a professional online presence.

This originally started out as a class project. I was taking Intro to Web Design and got a basic rundown of how things worked. It paved the building blocks. I spent weeks on this, making it more and more complex. I had a lot of fun remembering what I named files and wondering why the images wouldn't show up because I was one letter off.

After that, I worked on the Articles section, learning to incorporate some JavaScript to have the articles all in one section instead of making a new HTML page for each one. It's still a work in progress, but the foundation is there for it to keep growing.

I want to help reach people and give them the opportunity to work with someone who is patient and kind and willing to teach and learn. My original goal was to become a teacher. As I went to college, that goal shifted, but in a competitive job market, I'm still willing to adapt to get closer and closer to my goals. I want to listen and learn from people along the way to find out what companies are after, what I can help people with in the workplace and at home. Not what I need to do, but what we can do.

There is always something to learn out there, and technology is always advancing in different ways. I want to share my knowledge and experiences with you.
