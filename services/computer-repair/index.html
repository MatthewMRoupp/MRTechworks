Find the cause of a slow computer

Low storage, startup applications, software problems, and aging hardware can all slow a PC down. Diagnosis helps identify which issue applies to your computer and whether repair makes sense.

Startup, software, and printer problems

Get help with Windows errors, applications that will not open, printer connections, and everyday device problems. Tell me when the issue started and whether anything changed just before it happened.

Repair or replace?

We can compare the likely repair cost with the age of your device and what you need it to do. If an upgrade is appropriate, compatibility and costs can be discussed before purchasing parts.

What does it cost?

General diagnostics start at $50. Standard labor is $50 per hour, with the diagnostic fee applied toward paid repair labor. Parts, software, licenses, and travel may cost extra. Contact me to discuss your situation and current availability.

Before you contact me

Share the device model, symptoms, any error messages, and what you want to achieve. Keep a backup of important files where possible. Please do not send passwords in your message.
