Separate Wi-Fi problems from internet problems

A weak wireless signal and a slow internet connection need different fixes. Comparing devices and wired versus wireless connections helps narrow down the cause.

Improve coverage where you use it

Router placement, walls, distance, and interference affect coverage. I can help review the layout and whether moving equipment, Ethernet, or a mesh system would fit your needs.

Connect your everyday devices

What does it cost?

General diagnostics start at $50. Standard labor is $50 per hour, with the diagnostic fee applied toward paid repair labor. Parts, software, licenses, and travel may cost extra. Contact me to discuss your situation and current availability.

Before you contact me
