Process

What Working With Me Is Meant to Feel Like

1

Understand the Issue

We start by creating a clear picture of what happened, when it started, and what may have changed. It may be a simple fix or a more complicated issue, but the first goal is understanding what is actually happening instead of guessing.

2

Explain the Options

If there are multiple solutions, I want you to understand the benefits, limitations, and expected cost of each option. Sometimes the least expensive fix makes sense. Other times, spending a little more may provide a better long-term result.

3

Work on the Solution

If there is a budget and a goal, I will work to find a practical solution that fits both. Once the work is complete, the system should be tested whenever possible to make sure the original problem has been addressed.

4

Leave Things Clearer

Good support should leave you more informed than when we started. I want you to understand what was done, why it mattered, and what you can watch for if a similar issue happens again.

Q & A

Common Questions

How much does service usually cost?

General diagnostics start at $25 and standard labor is $50 per hour. Parts, software, licenses, and travel may cost extra when required. If the diagnostic turns into a larger job, I will communicate with you about the expected additional cost before significant work continues.

Does the $25 diagnostic fee get added on top of the repair?

If you choose to continue with paid repair work, the $25 diagnostic fee is applied toward the labor charge rather than simply being added on top of it.

Will you buy parts without asking me first?

Not normally. If a repair requires a part, license, subscription, or another paid item, I want you to know the expected cost before I purchase it whenever practical.

Can you guarantee that every computer can be repaired?

No. Some devices have failed hardware, severe damage, unavailable parts, corrupted data, or other problems that make a repair impractical. The goal of diagnosis is to determine what is wrong and give you realistic options rather than promise a repair that may not make sense.

Are these services meant to show current skills or future business direction?

Both. This page represents what I can already speak to and help with while also showing the direction I want MR Techworks to continue growing toward.

Do you only focus on advanced technical work?

No. Some of the most helpful work is basic support done clearly and patiently. A lot of IT support comes down to understanding the person's needs, explaining the problem, and providing good customer service.

Why does my Wi-Fi work in one room but not another?

Wi-Fi can be affected by distance, walls, floors, appliances, old equipment, and router placement. Sometimes the problem is not your internet provider at all, but the way the signal moves through your home.

I want to help identify where the problem is actually coming from before you spend money on upgrades you may not need.

Do I need a new computer, or can mine still be fixed?

Not every slow computer needs to be replaced. Sometimes a cleanup, storage upgrade, software fix, or better understanding of what is running in the background can make a big difference.

My goal is to help determine whether your device still has life left in it or whether replacing it would genuinely be the better choice.

Why is my computer so slow?

A slow computer can be caused by low storage, too many startup programs, outdated hardware, background apps, malware, or simply age. The important part is not guessing.

I want to look at what is actually causing the slowdown and explain whether it appears to be a small fix, a larger issue, or a sign that it may be time to upgrade.

How do I know if something is a scam?

Scams often create pressure. They may claim your account is locked, your device is infected, or you need to act immediately. Be especially cautious when someone unexpectedly demands gift cards, payment, passwords, or remote access.

I can help you review suspicious situations and look for warning signs before you click, reply, provide access, or make a payment.

Is it safe to let someone remote into my computer?

Remote support can be appropriate when you know who you are working with and you intentionally gave permission. It becomes much more concerning when an unexpected caller, message, or pop-up demands immediate remote access.

What should I do before buying a new laptop or computer?

Think about what you actually need it for: school, work, gaming, video calls, storage, travel, creative work, or basic browsing. A more expensive computer is not automatically the right computer.

I can help compare options based on your real needs and budget instead of pushing you toward features you may never use.

Why does my device keep running out of storage?

Storage fills up from photos, videos, downloads, old files, applications, backups, and sometimes hidden system data. Deleting random files may help, but it can also cause problems if you remove something important.

Should I repair my device or replace it?

That depends on the age of the device, the repair cost, current performance, and what you need it to do. Sometimes repairing is clearly worthwhile. Other times, putting more money into an old device may not make financial sense.

I want to give honest advice even when that means recommending that you do not spend money on a repair.

Why does my printer never work when I need it?

Printers are weird little drama machines. Common issues include Wi-Fi problems, drivers, paper jams, low ink or toner, incorrect default printer settings, or the printer being connected to a different network.

Checking the simple things first usually saves everyone some suffering.

Can you teach me how to fix small problems myself?

Yes. I do not want you to feel helpless with your own technology. Some repairs should be handled carefully, but there are plenty of small things you can safely learn to check yourself.

I want to explain what I am doing, why I am doing it, and what signs to look for next time.

Why does my phone, computer, or tablet keep asking for updates?

Updates often fix bugs, improve security, and help applications continue working correctly. They can be annoying, but ignoring them for too long can cause other problems later.

What information should I back up?

Important documents, photos, videos, work or school files, passwords or recovery information, and anything else you would be upset to lose should have a backup. Devices can usually be replaced. Personal data often cannot.

Why does my computer say it has a virus?

Sometimes the warning is legitimate. Other times it is a fake browser pop-up designed to scare you. Avoid clicking unexpected warnings until you understand where they came from.

Why should I ask for tech help instead of just Googling it?

Searching online can be useful, but it can also lead to outdated advice, confusing instructions, unnecessary purchases, and risky downloads. The internet can give you an answer without knowing whether that answer actually applies to your situation.

What makes your tech help different?

I want my services to feel clear, honest, and useful. I am not just trying to fix something and leave. I want you to understand what happened, what was done, and what it means for your device or setup.

Good tech support should leave you with a working solution and a little more confidence than you had before.

Every situation is different. A quick conversation is often the easiest way to determine what kind of help makes sense and whether MR Techworks is a good fit for the job.
