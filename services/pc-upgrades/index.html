Choose upgrades that fit your computer

More memory or an SSD may help some systems, but compatibility comes first. Share your computer model, current specifications, and the applications or games you want to use.

Plan a gaming or everyday PC

Get help comparing processors, graphics cards, storage, power supplies, and cooling. The goal is a balanced parts list for your actual use, rather than spending more on every component.

Understand the whole cost

Parts and software are separate from labor. We can discuss what is reusable, what needs replacing, and whether a prebuilt computer would be a better fit.

What does it cost?

General diagnostics start at $50. Standard labor is $50 per hour, with the diagnostic fee applied toward paid repair labor. Parts, software, licenses, and travel may cost extra. Contact me to discuss your situation and current availability.

Before you contact me

Share the device model, symptoms, any error messages, and what you want to achieve. Keep a backup of important files where possible. Please do not send passwords in your message.
