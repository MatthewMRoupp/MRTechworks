# MR Techworks real photograph reuse

Contains 99 actual photos, machine-checked at download time against CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA. Individual creator, source and original license are in NEWS_PHOTO_CREDITS.csv. Copyright and trademark rights of other content depicted in the photos may remain. For CC BY-SA adaptations, distribute the adapted photographs on the same license, include author/source/license links and state that the image was resized/cropped. Photographs must be visually reviewed for accurate product identity before placement in an article.
Eleven misleading filenames were corrected and two unrelated/mistaken results were replaced.
