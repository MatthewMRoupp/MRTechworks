Every converted image includes cropping or padding; preserve creator credit, source, linked CC license, modification note and CC BY-SA share-alike condition where applicable. Do not present an illustrated PC as a named console, or an unrelated photo as evidence of a specific outage. Do not replace approved license information with a generic © MR Techworks claim.
