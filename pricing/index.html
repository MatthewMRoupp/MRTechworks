Computer repair pricing & general estimates

Know the starting cost before handing over your computer. These rates help you plan; I’ll discuss your device, the work involved, and the expected charges before starting.

Simple service rates

Appointments are arranged around evening and weekend availability.

Diagnostic & evaluation

$50

Includes up to one hour investigating one reported computer issue, an explanation of findings, and recommended next steps. Credited toward hourly repair labor for the same issue.

Standard repair labor

$50/hour

For work completed by arrangement without a home visit. A $50 minimum applies to jobs requiring diagnosis; additional time is billed in 15-minute increments at $12.50.

Remote support

$25 minimum

Includes the first 30 minutes of an agreed remote session. Additional time is $12.50 per 15 minutes. Suitable for software and setup issues when the device can connect.

In-home support

$75 first hour

$50/hour afterward, billed in 15-minute increments. Available by appointment for agreed local visits. Any additional travel charge is quoted before booking.

How the diagnostic credit works

The $50 diagnostic is part of your labor total for the same issue. It is not an extra $50 added to that total. Extended diagnosis beyond the included hour requires your approval and is billed at the standard rate.

Example: two hours of diagnosis and repair

One hour diagnosing the issue plus one hour repairing it equals $100 total labor. If you already paid the $50 diagnostic, the remaining labor balance is $50. Parts and applicable tax are separate.

If you decline the repair or repair is impractical, the $50 diagnostic still covers the evaluation performed. If the issue is resolved within that initial hour, total labor is $50. A paid remote session or in-home visit uses its own rate above; an additional diagnostic fee is not automatically stacked on top.
