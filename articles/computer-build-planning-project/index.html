Computer Build Planning and Hardware Recommendations

By Matthew Roupp · MR Techworks · Article published November 23, 2026

Compatibility checking, budgeting, and explaining tradeoffs clearly.

I enjoy comparing parts and creating realistic recommendations that balance price, upgrade potential, and performance for the person actually using the machine. Everyone has different needs and budgets when choosing a computer.

There are a lot of options available, and I have spent time learning the ins and outs of computer components and what they do. I find the technical side fascinating, but most people simply want answers to practical questions: Is it powerful enough to run the games or programs I need? Will it last a long time before it needs to be upgraded?

I want to help people make sure they are getting what they actually need without being overwhelmed by confusing specifications or unnecessary upgrades.

What it shows

  • Structured hardware research and comparison
  • Decision-making based on goals instead of hype alone
  • The ability to explain technical choices in plain language
