Microsoft 365 · Incident report

Outlook and other Microsoft 365 services disrupted November 12–13

Microsoft reported problems reaching Exchange Online and functions that depend on it, including Outlook and certain Teams and Copilot experiences. In the latest service-health update checked on November 13 at 11:02 AM Eastern, Microsoft was still monitoring residual impact and recovery.

Microsoft listed service degradation for incident MO1550821, and its reporting did not establish that all affected customers were restored. Some users may have intermittent access or delayed email delivery.
