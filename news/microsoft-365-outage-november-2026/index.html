Microsoft 365 outage affects Outlook and related services

Published November 13, 2026 · MR Techworks · Source status checked at 11:20 AM Eastern on November 13

A Microsoft 365 incident that began Thursday, November 12, caused widespread problems accessing Outlook, Exchange Online and connected Microsoft cloud functions. Microsoft reported improving availability on Friday, but recovery was not uniform.

What was affected?

Microsoft's service-health records identify MO1550821, involving multiple Microsoft 365 services, and EX1550543, involving Exchange Online mailbox connectivity. Users could have difficulty accessing Outlook desktop or webmail, and some messages were delayed. Features in Teams and other services that rely on Exchange Online could also experience problems.

The incident was not the same as every user's entire computer or internet connection failing. Different applications and organizations experienced different symptoms.

What did Microsoft say about recovery?

As of Microsoft's 11:02 AM Eastern status update on November 13, the multi-service incident was still listed as service degradation. Microsoft said it was working through residual effects and checking that recovery remained stable across connection methods. The update was not a declaration of full restoration for every customer.

The Associated Press reported improving email delivery and availability across affected regions while warning that some customers might still have intermittent access or delayed messages. Microsoft also reported no evidence of malicious activity associated with the incident at that time. A final post-incident investigation could provide additional details.

What should users do?

  1. Check the provider first. Microsoft 365 administrators should view Health → Service health in the Microsoft 365 admin center for tenant-specific details.
  2. Do not assume reinstalling Office will fix a cloud problem. Repeated local changes during a provider incident can make troubleshooting harder.
  3. Use an approved alternate communication method for time-sensitive messages while mail is disrupted.
  4. Test again after recovery. If only one device still fails, investigate that device separately.

For more detailed checks, read Is It an Outage, Your Internet, or Your Device?
