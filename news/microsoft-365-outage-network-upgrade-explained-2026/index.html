Microsoft explains November 365 outage after service restoration

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026

Microsoft reported global Microsoft 365 service restoration on November 14. Its preliminary account connects the disruption to an earlier network-capacity upgrade and a subsequent network change.

What is confirmed

Microsoft said the incident was technical, not a cyberattack or AI-agent failure. Recovery was gradual while engineers monitored stability and delayed mail queues. Organizations should review tenant Service health and verify mail delivery. A complete post-incident report was still pending. No separate Corning or Elmira incident was established.

What it means

This is a dated news report, not a claim about unannounced features or availability. For changes after publication, consult the original announcement.
