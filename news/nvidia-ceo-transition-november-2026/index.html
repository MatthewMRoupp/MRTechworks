NVIDIA CEO Transition: What Is Confirmed and What Comes Next

MR Techworks · Published November 13, 2026 · Reporting current through November 13, 2026

On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA publicly announced the resignation of co-founder Jensen Huang as chief executive and board member, with finance chief Colette Kress named interim CEO.

The official leadership changes

According to NVIDIA, Huang resigned as president, CEO and a board director, effective immediately. Colette Kress remains chief financial officer while taking on interim CEO responsibilities. NVIDIA's board appointed Harvey C. Jones as chair and began a search for a permanent CEO.

NVIDIA filed the departure and appointments with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Its press release is dated November 12; the SEC filing records the board's actions on November 11.

What is—and is not—known about NVIDIA products

Leadership changes at a major chip supplier can matter to gaming graphics cards, AI systems and the semiconductor ecosystem, but the departure itself does not prove that products will be canceled, prices will rise or driver support will stop.

NVIDIA says its leadership team intends to continue the company's technology roadmap and customer commitments. That is the company's stated plan, not a guarantee of any particular future outcome.

What to watch

NVIDIA has scheduled its next earnings report for November 17, 2026. Subsequent board announcements, product roadmaps and official financial disclosures should be the main sources for updates.

Reports about the circumstances of the resignation should be attributed carefully and kept distinct from officially established facts and unresolved allegations.
