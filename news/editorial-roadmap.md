## Editorial policy

1. Prioritize material current news, verified service disruptions, critical updates, and major platform changes. Keep local consumer impact clear.
2. For historical recaps, label the **original event or source date** separately from the **actual publication date**. Do not falsify timestamps to make a new piece appear old.
3. Use official primary sources wherever possible, cite them within articles, and identify reporting versus speculation.
4. Do not generate new articles from unverified claims. Confirm links and date-specific details immediately before publication.
5. Make guides actionable, accessible, and connected to related news. Avoid near-duplicate SEO filler.
6. Use licensed or original visuals with meaningful alt text; label AI-generated depictions accurately.
7. Review pages for mobile layout, navigation, structured metadata, SEO, source links, and stale information.
