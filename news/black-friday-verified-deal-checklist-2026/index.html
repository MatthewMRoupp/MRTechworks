Black Friday 2026 Deal Check: What Counts as a Real Discount?

MR Techworks · Published November 27, 2026 · Updated November 28, 2026

A crossed-out price is a marketing comparison, not proof that today's offer is the lowest available. For Black Friday on November 27, use a consistent way to check the actual item, seller and full cost. This guide deliberately does not label specific products as “verified deals” without independently checked price histories.

The six checks that make a deal worth considering

  1. Match the exact SKU. Similar laptop, router or TV names can hide differences in processor, memory, storage, screen, included accessories or warranty. Compare the manufacturer model number, not just a product family.
  2. Look beyond the reference price. Compare current prices at multiple reputable stores. Where a price-history tracker has data, inspect prior weeks or months, noting that third-party histories may omit coupons or membership prices.
  3. Verify the seller. On marketplaces, examine who actually sells and ships the item. Search unfamiliar businesses by name and web address, including independent complaints. A familiar platform does not make every third-party seller equally reliable.
  4. Calculate the delivered price. Shipping, taxes, activation charges, subscriptions and necessary accessories can erase the headline discount. Check final checkout totals before paying.
  5. Read returns and warranty terms. A final-sale or refurbished item may have narrower protections. Read the return window, restocking fees, manufacturer's warranty and who handles support.
  6. Use an appropriate payment method. The FTC recommends credit cards when possible for stronger shopping protections. Avoid sellers demanding gift cards, wire transfers, cryptocurrency or unusual payment-app transfers.

Examples of misleading comparisons

A 2 TB SSD marked “40% off” might be a different model from the one you checked last month. A mesh kit might omit the faster Ethernet ports you need. A laptop may display a large reference discount while shipping with less memory than a nearly identically named configuration. These are examples, not allegations about particular sellers.

How to calculate your real savings before Cyber Monday

FTC pricing guidance distinguishes a genuine previous asking price from an artificially inflated comparison price. A manufacturer’s suggested price is also not necessarily what shoppers usually pay. For example, if a router is advertised at $120, reduced from $200, the advertised saving is 40%. If the same model was regularly available for $130, the saving against that observed baseline is only about 7.7%. These figures are illustrative, not actual offers.

  1. Record the exact model, seller, condition and current delivered price.
  2. Look at 30–90 days of available price history, including ordinary non-holiday prices.
  3. Compare the same configuration at two or more reputable retailers.
  4. Subtract the current delivered price from your comparable baseline, then divide by that baseline and multiply by 100. Account for coupons, memberships and shipping consistently.
  5. Save dated screenshots and repeat the comparison on November 30. Missing price history is uncertainty, not proof of deception.

What we can actually verify today

We have verified consumer-protection guidance from the FTC and cross-checked the principles against the existing MR Techworks shopping safety coverage. We have not independently validated a dated, seller-specific shortlist of discounts, so this page does not invent sale prices, availability, coupons or rankings. Any future product recommendation should record the exact SKU, seller, checked-at time, listed price, shipping and price-history comparison.

Before clicking a sponsored result

The FTC warned on November 19, 2026 that scammers can use deceptive shopping search results to direct customers to imitation retailers. Instead of following a suspicious ad, navigate to a retailer you already know or type its address yourself. Read our fake stores and reviews guide for more warning signs.
