# MR Techworks public brand voice

This is the copy and contact convention for customer-facing pages, service descriptions, FAQs, ticket confirmation pages, and future website updates.

## Public contact

- **Primary business email:** `support@mrtechworks.com`
- Phone/text: `607-346-9349`, with text preferred for initial contact.
- Do not show `matthew@mrtechworks.com` as the public contact address, except when a document specifically needs an individually identified professional address.
- The support inbox is an existing Google Workspace group/send-as identity, not a separate licensed employee mailbox.

## Brand language

- Write as **MR Techworks** using **we**, **us**, and **our** in customer-facing support, service, pricing, follow-up, and contact copy.
- Be friendly, clear, local, and professional. Avoid generic corporate jargon or exaggerated claims about staff, availability, certifications, response times, or capabilities.
- MR Techworks is owner-operated. Do not imply an unverified team of technicians or 24/7 staffed support.
- Prefer "We'll follow up about scheduling" rather than suggesting an automatic booking or guaranteed same-day response.
- Keep biography, author bylines, certifications, college work, and personal projects in **first person** when Matthew is speaking about his individual experience.

## Reusable examples

- "Tell us what's happening and we'll help you plan the next step."
- "Contact MR Techworks at support@mrtechworks.com."
- "We offer appointments by arrangement, primarily weekday evenings and weekends."
- "We'll review your request and follow up. Submitting a ticket does not book an appointment."

## Scope of this update

The website's public email links, structured business contact data, service pages and common footers use the support inbox. This does **not** change the Google Workspace account's default reply or Gmail send-as settings. Those remain controlled through Google Workspace/Gmail.

The public help-ticket backend and notification flow are not modified by this brand-copy update.
