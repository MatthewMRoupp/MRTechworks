Sony expands QSSR upscaling to more PlayStation 5 games

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026 · Original reporting dates cited below

Sony says QSSR updates are rolling out for Spider-Man 2, The Finals, Arc Raiders and more.

What's changing

Sony announced on November 13 that Quick Spectral Super Resolution, or QSSR, is expanding to additional PlayStation 5 games. The list includes Marvel's Spider-Man 2, The Finals, Arc Raiders, No Man's Sky, Control Resonant and Hell Let Loose: Vietnam.

What QSSR does

Sony describes QSSR as AI-guided image upscaling designed to improve perceived sharpness, edge quality and stability compared with traditional techniques. It is related to PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution technology used on PS5 Pro.

When to expect it

Sony said updates would roll out over the coming weeks. That does not mean every named game already has the feature today, or that every game will show the same visual improvement. Check individual game patch notes before comparing image quality.
