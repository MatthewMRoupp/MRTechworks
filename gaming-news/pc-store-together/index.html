PC Store Together: Run your own computer repair shop with friends

November 23, 2026 · By MR Techworks · Research-based preview

Building computers, troubleshooting hardware and helping customers might sound like a normal day at MR Techworks. PC Store Together brings that concept to a cooperative business simulation from Sykes Games and Nightmare Studios.

From a small shop to a technology business

The game's advertised premise combines computer assembly, hardware troubleshooting, customer service and shop management. Players can work alone or cooperate with friends, dividing responsibilities such as building PCs, serving customers and managing stock. These are advertised features, not features we have independently tested.

Why the launch is worth watching

The game was scheduled for November 23. Launch-delay reporting indicates that the final build was awaiting Steam review. We have not independently confirmed a revised release date or final price. Check the official store listing for current availability and any demo.

Before you buy

Check the current system requirements on Steam, especially graphics hardware, memory and storage. If you plan to play with friends, confirm multiplayer support and whether everyone needs a copy. With Black Friday approaching, do not assume an introductory discount is the best available price. Wait for independent player reviews if you are unsure.

Can a simulator teach real computer repair?

A game can encourage curiosity about components and troubleshooting, but it cannot replace real technical training. Actual repair requires electrical safety, electrostatic discharge precautions, manufacturer instructions and careful protection of customer data.
