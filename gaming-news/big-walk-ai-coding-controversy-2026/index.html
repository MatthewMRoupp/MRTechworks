Big Walk AI disclosure raises questions about indie-game awards

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026

House House disclosed that it used chatbot assistance sparingly to solve a technical problem in Big Walk. The studio estimates the resulting code represents well under 0.1% of the game.

What is confirmed

Some Independent Games Festival jurors object to generative AI in award submissions. That position should not be mistaken for a festival-wide ruling. House House says it will develop a clearer internal AI policy; its disclosure does not mean the game was entirely AI-generated.

What it means
