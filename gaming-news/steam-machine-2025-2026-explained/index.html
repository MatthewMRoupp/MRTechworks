Valve's New Steam Machine: From the 2025 Announcement to 2026 Reservations

By MR Techworks · Published November 13, 2026

Original event: November 12, 2025 — Valve announces new Steam hardware

Valve's November 12, 2025 hardware announcement included the Steam Machine, Steam Controller and Steam Frame headset. During 2026, Steam Machine reservations and announced prices turned that proposal into a more concrete option for living-room PC gaming.

Why the Steam Machine matters

Valve's Steam Machine is a compact, SteamOS-based PC designed to make playing Steam games on a television or desktop more approachable. The platform connects to a user's existing Steam library instead of requiring a new, exclusive console storefront.

SteamOS is Linux-based. This matters for compatibility: individual Windows-only applications, anti-cheat systems and game launchers may require checking before replacing an existing Windows gaming PC.

What Valve announced in 2026

Valve's June 22, 2026 announcement introduced reservations for 512GB and 2TB configurations, including controller bundles. The announced US prices began at $1,049 for the 512GB version and $1,349 for the 2TB version. These are historical launch prices, not current stock or pricing claims.

Valve described a reservation allocation system intended to reduce the advantage of automated purchase tools. Read current Valve product and reservation pages before trying to order.

Hardware and practical trade-offs

Valve's specifications list a semi-custom six-core AMD Zen 4 CPU, an RDNA 3 GPU, 16GB DDR5 memory and 8GB GDDR6 graphics memory. Connectivity includes Wi-Fi 6E and gigabit Ethernet. Its listed models use 512GB or 2TB NVMe storage.

The small-form-factor design and SteamOS interface appeal to players who want PC games in a couch-friendly setup. A traditional desktop still offers different upgrade and Windows software options. Display output capabilities should not be confused with guaranteed frame rates in every game.

A one-year retrospective

The transition from a 2025 announcement to 2026 reservation options is an important example of Valve expanding its hardware beyond Steam Deck. More specific conclusions about long-term performance and value require measurements and availability data rather than relying only on promotional claims.
