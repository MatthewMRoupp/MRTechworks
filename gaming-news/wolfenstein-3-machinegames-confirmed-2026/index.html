Wolfenstein 3 confirmed: MachineGames is developing the next chapter

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026

Xbox confirmed on November 12 that Wolfenstein 3 is in production at MachineGames. Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson will return as BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker. No release date was announced.

What is confirmed

This announcement confirms development, not a launch window, platform lineup or gameplay features. Wait for MachineGames or Xbox to announce those details.

What it means

This is a dated news report, not a claim about unannounced features or availability. For changes after publication, consult the original announcement.
