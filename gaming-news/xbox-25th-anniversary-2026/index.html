Xbox Turns 25: Original Xbox Games Reach Windows PCs

MR Techworks · Published November 13, 2026 · Reporting current through November 13, 2026

Microsoft marked the Xbox platform's 25th anniversary on November 12, 2026, with a broader backward-compatibility library for Windows PCs and a collection of game announcements.

Twenty-five original Xbox games now available on PC

Microsoft added 21 original Xbox titles to its PC backward-compatibility collection, raising the total to 25. The additions include Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind, and TimeSplitters 2.

Microsoft says the supported PC offering requires Windows 11 and is limited to select markets. Depending on the title, a purchase or eligible subscription may be required. This does not mean every original Xbox game is now playable on PC.

New achievements and anniversary announcements

Achievement support was added for four classic games, including Knights of the Old Republic and Conker: Live & Reloaded. Microsoft also highlighted new Fable gameplay, a Firefight update for Halo: Campaign Evolved, and the announcement that Wolfenstein 3 is in production.

These announcements cover different stages of development and availability. A game being in production does not establish a release date.

Why this matters

Backward compatibility can preserve access to older titles and reduce the need to keep a legacy console working. Players should check an individual game's compatibility, storefront eligibility, and save support before purchasing.

For PC gamers, Windows version and account licensing matter at least as much as the headline title list.

November 14 update: 25 original Xbox games on Windows PC

Xbox says it added 21 original Xbox titles to PC backward compatibility on November 12, bringing the total to 25. Examples include Morrowind, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic, Republic Commando and TimeSplitters 2. Four earlier games were already available; not all 25 are included in Game Pass. Windows 11, a digital purchase or eligible subscription, and supported-region availability may apply. Microsoft also added achievements to select classic titles.
