PS5 QSSR upscaling comes to Spider-Man 2 and more games

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026

Sony announced additional Quick Spectral Super Resolution support on November 13. Insomniac says the Marvel's Spider-Man 2 update is live, while other games are receiving support later.

What is confirmed

Arc Raiders, The Finals and No Man's Sky are among planned updates; Remedy expects Control Resonant support in December. QSSR aims to improve image clarity and stability, but results and availability depend on the game. Do not treat planned updates as already released.

What it means

This is a dated news report, not a claim about unannounced features or availability. For changes after publication, consult the original announcement.
