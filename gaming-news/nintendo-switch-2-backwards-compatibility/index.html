Nintendo Switch 2 Compatibility: Which Original Switch Games and Controllers Work?

By MR Techworks · Published November 13, 2026

Upgrading from the original Nintendo Switch does not necessarily mean abandoning your existing game collection. Nintendo says Switch 2 plays many compatible Switch games, although certain titles and accessories have exceptions.

Original Switch games

Nintendo says Switch 2 supports compatible physical and digital games designed for the first Switch. Some titles may not start or may have in-game compatibility issues because the new hardware differs from the original system.

Check Nintendo's compatibility information for each must-play game instead of assuming the entire library works. For non-Nintendo releases, the game publisher may provide additional compatibility information.

Digital libraries and saved progress

Keep access to your Nintendo Account and verify which save-transfer or cloud-save features your particular games use. Ownership, game compatibility and saved progress are related but separate issues.

Do not erase or sell an old console until you have tested the games and important saves on the new system. Some games have additional transfer requirements.

Controllers and accessories

Nintendo says original Joy-Con controllers can pair wirelessly with Switch 2 but cannot attach directly to it. The original Nintendo Switch Pro Controller is supported for compatible uses, with details and limitations listed by Nintendo.

Check specialty controllers, chargers, docks and peripherals individually. A connector that physically fits does not prove a device is supported.

Updates and upgrade editions

Some games receive free improvements or paid upgrades on Switch 2. The ability to launch an older game does not automatically mean improved resolution or frame rate. Use the game's official store listing for specific upgrade features.

Before upgrading, inventory your games and accessories, verify account access, and check Nintendo's support pages for the latest compatibility findings.
