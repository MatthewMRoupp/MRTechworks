Nintendo Switch 2 Sales Reach 26.35 Million: What the Figure Means

MR Techworks · Published November 13, 2026 · Reporting current through November 13, 2026

Nintendo's investor-relations figures list 26.35 million Nintendo Switch 2 hardware units sold worldwide as of September 30, 2026.

The reported numbers

Nintendo lists 26.35 million cumulative Switch 2 hardware units and 73.21 million cumulative software units through September 30, 2026. These are global life-to-date figures, not sales for September alone.

Nintendo's top-selling-title data lists 16.48 million copies of Mario Kart World for the same reporting date. Nintendo notes that its software sales totals can include copies bundled with hardware.

How to interpret the milestone

Sales figures provide one signal of a console's adoption, but they do not independently tell us how many people play daily, whether stores have inventory locally, or how well every new game will perform.

Comparisons to other consoles need consistent time windows: launch-to-date sales versus launch-to-date sales, not a new system's totals versus an older system's lifetime sales.

What to watch next

The next meaningful checks are Nintendo's later investor updates, software launches, hardware availability, and any official revisions to forecasts. These can change the picture of the console's growth.
