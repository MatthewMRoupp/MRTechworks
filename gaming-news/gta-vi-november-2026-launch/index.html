GTA VI: November 19 Launch, Pre-Loading, and Confirmed Platforms

MR Techworks · Published November 13, 2026 · Reporting current through November 13, 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for November 19, 2026. Digital pre-loading began November 10, according to Rockstar's official announcement.

What is confirmed

Rockstar lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the game's native launch platforms. The publisher says the current-generation console versions target 30 frames per second. It also describes additional visual features on PlayStation 5 Pro, including improved image clarity through PSSR and ray-traced shadows.

Rockstar's November 10 announcement says digital pre-loading is available to players who pre-ordered. Pre-loading downloads game files early; it does not grant access before the release date.

Will GTA VI work on Xbox One or PC?

Xbox has separately confirmed day-one cloud streaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series consoles through Xbox Cloud Gaming. Cloud streaming is not the same as running GTA VI locally on Xbox One; performance depends on connection quality and service requirements.

As of this report, Rockstar's launch page does not list a Windows PC edition. A PC release date or minimum PC specification would be speculation rather than a confirmed fact.

A practical launch checklist

Check your console's storage space, confirm which edition you own, enable automatic updates or pre-loading, and verify your account can sign in before launch day. For cloud play, test connection quality rather than relying only on the advertised download speed.

We will revisit this story after launch with any verified performance changes, patches, or confirmed platform news.

November 14 update: companies offer gaming time off

With Rockstar still listing November 19 as the GTA VI launch date for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, Logitech announced a flexible extra gaming day for employees. Pocketpair also announced time off around the launch for staff who plan to play. Logitech's benefit is for a game of employees' choice, not a requirement to play GTA VI. These company policies do not imply a PC release date.
