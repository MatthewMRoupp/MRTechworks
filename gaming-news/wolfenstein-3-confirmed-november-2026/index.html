Wolfenstein 3 confirmed: MachineGames is working on the next game

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026 · Original reporting dates cited below

Xbox confirms Wolfenstein 3 is in production, but has not announced a release date or platforms.

What Xbox announced

During its November 12 Xbox anniversary announcements, Microsoft confirmed that Wolfenstein 3 is officially in production at MachineGames. Xbox also identified Brian Bloom and Debra Wilson as returning performers for BJ Blazkowicz and Grace Walker.

What remains unknown

Xbox's announcement did not give a release date, launch platforms, price or a detailed gameplay reveal. It is too early to treat any rumored launch window as confirmed.

Why it matters

The announcement establishes that the series is continuing. Players should watch MachineGames and Xbox's official channels for footage and platform details instead of treating fan speculation as a release schedule.

Sources and scope

  • Xbox Wire — 25th anniversary announcements, November 12, 2026
