ARC Raiders Black Friday: $19.99 on Steam, but is it a new low?

November 24, 2026 · By MR Techworks · Price check, not a hands-on review

Embark Studios is discounting its multiplayer extraction game for Black Friday. The US Steam listing shows the base game at $19.99 on November 24, down from $39.99. But a previous promotion reached the same price, so there is no evidence that this is a new record.

What Steam buyers actually pay

  • Base game: $19.99 (regular $39.99).
  • Deluxe: $29.99 (regular $59.99).
  • Deluxe upgrade for existing owners: $9.99 (regular $19.99).

These are US dollar storefront prices before applicable tax, checked November 24. Steam lists December 3 as the end date. The Deluxe package adds cosmetic content and in-game currency; the base game is enough to start playing.

Different stores, different deadlines

  • PS5: November 16–30, base game only.
  • Epic: November 17–December 1, Deluxe and Deluxe upgrade only.
  • Steam and Xbox Series X|S: November 19–December 3, all three editions or upgrades.

Embark announces a 50% reduction for those eligible products. We have not independently verified exact US checkout prices outside Steam. Check your own store before buying; local pricing and end times may differ.

Is this a genuine bargain?

Steam Dashboard recorded the US base game dropping to $19.99 on October 2 and returning to $39.99 on October 16. Its November records show the same discount returning. This matches the lowest price observed in its tracking window beginning July 2, not necessarily the lowest ever. Buy because you want to play, not because the banner creates urgency.

What is new today?

Embark's November 24 notice says update 2.7.0 is rolling out with a Raider Project and the Nessuno Set. The game supports cross-platform multiplayer and parties of up to three, with both cooperative and competitive encounters. It requires an internet connection and an Embark account. This is source-based coverage, not a gameplay review.

Check your PC before spending

Steam lists 12 GB of memory as the minimum and 16 GB as recommended, alongside DirectX 12 and broadband. Compare your actual CPU and GPU with the official requirements rather than assuming a gaming label guarantees performance.
