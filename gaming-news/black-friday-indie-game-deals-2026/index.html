Black Friday indie gaming: one free co-op game and a $4.94 RPG

November 27, 2026 · By MR Techworks · Source-based buying guide

You do not need a new console or a huge budget to enjoy Black Friday. Two independent PC games offer very different experiences this weekend: a demanding cooperative climb and a fantasy adventure built around exploration and skills. One is reported free to keep and the other is listed at $4.94 on Steam.

Bread & Fred: a cooperative challenge for two

Neowin and Game*Spark report that Epic is giving away SandCastles Studio's platformer through December 3. PC Gamer lists its regular price as $14.99. Epic's general free-games page did not expose the specific offer in our direct check, so confirm the $0 checkout price before claiming it.

The two penguin characters are connected by a rope. Players coordinate jumps and use momentum to get across gaps; mistakes can send both tumbling down the mountain. Local and online co-op are reported supported. There is also a solo mode using a rock as a partner. This may appeal to friends who enjoy precision platforming, but it is not necessarily a relaxing game.

Visit the Epic free-games page, sign in and check the offer. A free-to-keep promotion is different from a free weekend: when successfully claimed, the game remains in your library after the promotion. Check the account library before leaving the store.

The Black Grimoire: Cursebreaker — $4.94 on Steam

Olipa Games' fantasy RPG reached full release on October 27, 2024, following Early Access in 2023. On November 27 the Steam listing showed $4.94, down from $14.99 (67% off), with the promotion ending November 29. That is a $10.05 reduction in US dollars before applicable taxes. We have not independently checked its complete historical pricing, so we are not calling this a record low.

The game draws on older online role-playing games for its approach to skill advancement. Combat, gathering and crafting provide different routes to character progression. Fishing, mining and cooking are among the available activities. Despite its online-game inspirations, it is a solo adventure: optional social interactions do not provide cooperative combat or trading.

A free demo is available on Steam. The store lists 8 GB of RAM and 4 GB of storage as minimum requirements. Although the game listing mentions Windows 7, Steam itself requires Windows 10 or newer. Try the demo on your own hardware before spending money; minimum specifications are not a performance guarantee.

Why Steam discounts have different deadlines

Valve's November 26 announcement confirms that its redesigned Discounts & Events page is receiving Black Friday promotion rather than hosting a separate official sale event. The seasonal presentation ends November 30 at 1 PM Eastern, but individual publishers can use different promotion dates. The next Steam-wide Winter Sale is scheduled for December 17 through January 4, 2027.

If a game is not at a price you are comfortable paying, add it to your wishlist and compare again in December. Use a price history tracker to compare the same edition, currency and storefront. Do not confuse an optional DLC bundle with the base game.

Before you buy

  • Confirm the final price, edition and expiration date on the official storefront.
  • Check whether your friends need their own copies and whether online or local multiplayer fits your plans.
  • Try available demos and verify system requirements before purchasing upgrades.
  • Use account protection and avoid unfamiliar sellers promising unusually cheap keys.
