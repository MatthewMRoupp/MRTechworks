Big Walk's AI disclosure sparks a debate about indie game awards

MR Techworks · Published November 14, 2026 · Original reporting dates cited below

House House says it used limited AI-assisted debugging on Big Walk; independent festival jurors are debating eligibility.

What the studio disclosed

House House said it consulted a language-model chatbot while investigating technical problems late in development of Big Walk. According to the studio's account as reported by Ars Technica, the resulting adapted code represented well under 0.1% of the game's code. The studio did not describe AI-generated art or dialogue.

Why the controversy started

Some Independent Games Festival jurors have publicly opposed voting for games using generative AI in any capacity. Their stance is not necessarily an official rule or a position held by every juror. The debate centers on whether AI-assisted debugging should be treated like AI-generated creative content.

What readers should know

The studio's disclosure is a reported fact; arguments about fairness, disclosure and award eligibility are opinions and policy questions. A claim that the game has been officially disqualified would go beyond the available evidence.

Sources and scope
